About the study

How governance and management shape long-term firm development.

The survey asks businesses about their governance structure, management practices, aims and aspirations, and social embeddedness. It compares practices across promoter-led and professionally run enterprises to identify what structures best support long-term firm development.

Findings will help academics and policymakers design evidence-based policies and governance frameworks suited to Indian firms, and help business leaders examine their own practices against the broader corporate environment.

Throughout the survey, “promoter” refers to the founder(s) or controlling owner(s) of the business and, where applicable, their family.

Confidentiality and your rights

Your responses are confidential and no firm is ever identified.

How data is handled

  • Confidential and treated anonymously under GDPR, the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and the London Business School Research Lab privacy statement
  • Collected under the legal basis of “public task”
  • Anonymised data may be deposited in a research data archive for future academic use under open-science practices

Your choices

  • Participation is voluntary
  • You may skip questions
  • You may withdraw, or ask for your data to be withdrawn, up until the point of analysis, with no penalty
  • No risks beyond those of daily life

How results are used

FAQ

Common questions.

Why was my company invited?

Your company is part of a research sample of Indian companies drawn from public corporate registry information across regions, sectors and size bands.

Who should answer?

The promoter, a director or a senior executive who knows the company’s governance and management.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Responses are analysed anonymously and no firm is named in any publication.

How long does it take?

About 15–20 minutes, in one sitting.

Can someone else at my company complete it?

Yes, a senior colleague with good knowledge of the business can complete it on the company’s behalf. Please submit only one response per company.

When will I receive the benchmark report?

After the study closes and the analysis is complete.

I don’t want further emails.

Reply “OPT OUT” to the invitation, use the opt-out link at the bottom of the email, or write to participate@survo.tech, and we will remove you promptly.
